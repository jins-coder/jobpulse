# ⚡ JobPulse :: ATS Application Copilot (Chrome Extension)

Eliminate manual copy-pasting forever. When you visit any employer job application page (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, LinkedIn, or custom company career portals), JobPulse automatically detects the form and fills your tailored details with **1 click in 0.2 seconds**.

---

## 🚀 How to Install in Google Chrome / Brave / Edge (Takes 10 Seconds)

1. Open **Google Chrome** (or Edge / Brave / Arc).
2. In the address bar, go to:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions
   ```
3. Toggle **Developer mode** in the top-right corner to **ON**.
4. Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top-left corner.
5. Select this folder:
   ```text
   e:\projects\vuetest\jobpulse-extension
   ```

That's it! The **JobPulse Copilot ⚡** extension is now installed in your browser.

---

## 🎯 How to Use:

1. In **JobPulse Web App**, find a role and click **"Apply on Official Site"**.
2. Your tailored **PDF Resume** is downloaded, and the company portal opens in a new tab.
3. Look at the bottom-right corner of the company's page:
   - A floating button appears: **`⚡ JobPulse Autofill Form`**
4. Click the button:
   - **First Name & Last Name** are automatically typed.
   - **Email & Phone Number** are automatically typed.
   - **LinkedIn, GitHub, and Portfolio URLs** are automatically filled.
   - **Tailored Cover Pitch** is automatically injected into the comments/cover letter box.
   - **Work Authorization** radio buttons ("Are you legally authorized to work?") are automatically answered.
5. Simply attach the downloaded PDF resume and click **Submit Application**!
